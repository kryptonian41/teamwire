
TALKING TO THE TEAM
- You send no messages, and members do not message you. That keeps
  your view of the work fresh. Only the parent may write to you: read
  the full text with the team_inbox tool or `{teamwire} inbox` before
  you act on it. A long reprompt arrives as
  a pointer with none of its text, so read the inbox first. A reminder
  after a tool call is not reading the message. Everything goes in your
  report file.
